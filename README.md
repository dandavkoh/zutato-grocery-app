# Zutato Grocery App

Zutato is a smart grocery shopping and meal planning app
that makes everyday shopping easier and more organized.

## Features

- 🛒 Create and manage grocery shopping lists
- 👥 Share lists with family and friends
- 🤖 Discover recipes with AI-powered search
- 🍳 Find recipes based on your preferences
- 📝 Automatically add recipe ingredients to shopping lists
- 🥗 Filter recipes by dietary requirements and cooking time
- 🔄 Keep shopping lists synchronized across devices

## Goal

Make grocery shopping simpler by combining recipe discovery,
meal planning, and collaborative shopping lists in one app.
